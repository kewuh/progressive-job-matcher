# Scoring jobs

Every job gets an honest `fit` from 0 to 100, a one-sentence `why`, and a short `gaps`. Score against the profile's `summary` and `prefs`, not a generic idea of a good job.

## Fit

| Fit | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 75–100 | Strong match: core skills, right level, right place, mission they care about. Apply soon. |
| 55–74 | Good match with one real gap (level, a missing skill, location, lower pay). Worth a look. |
| 35–54 | Adjacent: transferable skills or strong mission but a weaker role fit. Include when the person wants an expansive list. |
| under 35 | Weak. Only include if they starred it or added it themselves. |

Weigh, roughly in this order:

1. **Skills overlap** with the profile: name the specific experience in `why` ("Ran the email/SMS program at X, which this role owns").
2. **Level:** one step above or below their current level is fine. Further away, lower the score and say so in gaps.
3. **Realistic odds:** if the profile says where they've had interviews (e.g. small, digital-first organizations) and where they've been rejected (e.g. famous institutions), score those accordingly and say so.
4. **Mission** alignment with the causes in the profile.
5. **Location:** apply `prefs` and `locations`. Secondary cities usually score about 5 points lower than the home area. Other places only if remote is allowed.
6. **Role type priorities** in prefs: e.g. press, social-only, major-gifts or grants roles may be lower priority for some people. Include them when the mission is strong, but score lower.

## Include or skip

- Default to **expansive**: include every level from coordinator to director, and fixed-term, contract, part-time and consulting roles at organizations they'd like. Never drop a good-mission role just for being junior or lower-paid; say so in gaps.
- Skip internships and student fellowships (unless prefs ask for them), roles in unrelated fields (pure finance, HR, IT, engineering, admin, unless the profile is in that field), roles the dealbreakers rule out, and clearly closed postings.
- Organizations the person marked as favorites (`kind: "org"` in watch, or noted in prefs): boost a good role by about 5–10 and say so.

## Writing why and gaps

- `why`: one sentence naming the matching experience. Not "great fit", but what fits.
- `gaps`: one short phrase on the main risk (level, missing tool, location, pay, big-institution odds). Empty if none.
- Write in the person's own spelling conventions (US or UK) and use their pronouns if you refer to them.
